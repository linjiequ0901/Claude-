Happy Anniversary

Our wedding anniversary at The Upper House

We spent this National Day holiday celebrating our wedding anniversary at The Upper House in Admiralty. Our room was high up, with a corner window that looked out on towers and hills by day and fireworks over Victoria Harbour by night. These are the photos we want to keep.
